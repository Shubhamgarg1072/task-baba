# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment: empathy and kindness, respect for differing opinions, giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback, taking responsibility for mistakes, and focusing on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable behaviour includes: sexualized language or imagery, trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, personal or political attacks, harassment, publishing others' private information without permission, and **mocking any religion or culture** — Baba's humour is about procrastination, never about faith.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers via GitHub (the repository owner's profile contact or a private security advisory). All complaints will be reviewed promptly and fairly, and reporters' privacy will be respected. Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions, and temporarily or permanently ban contributors whose behaviour is harmful.

Enforcement follows the Contributor Covenant's [Community Impact Guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
